Sharon Musa

I'm a Master's student in the CVIL Lab at York University, advised by Kosta Derpanis. My work sits at the intersection of computer vision and interpretability, researching the understanding of video models.

I completed my BSc in Computer Science at York University, where my undergraduate thesis on audio-video scene recognition was supervised by Richard P. Wildes. I also interned at the IVRL lab at EPFL, where I worked on 3D scene reconstruction from video, and as a Machine Learning Fellow at the AI4Good Lab.

Research interests: computer vision, video models, interpretability, XR/VR/AR, Responsible AI
